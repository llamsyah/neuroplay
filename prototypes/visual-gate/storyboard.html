Entry before → during → after: start from the selected surface and its Quick Insight. Across approximately 540–600 ms, the light staging deepens, the general introduction recedes, the anatomy viewport moves into Explorer, and the contextual content changes to the selected structure. The real canvas remains mounted in this isolated implementation. A frozen midpoint composition is available as a visual reference; it is not an exact animation capture.

Orientation: a directly picked visible region retains the current viewing direction on entry. Menu selection of a hidden opposite-side structure can introduce a deliberate orbit to expose it. Orbital interpolation stays around the target rather than moving the camera through the brain. Production continuity and perceived motion quality remain unverified.

Switching anatomy: the chosen node, selected highlight, description, and focus update together. No geometry moves. Choosing a surface while in hippocampus isolation restores whole anatomical context; it does not pretend that surface region has its own internal reveal.

Guided depth remains available: select Hipokampus in Explorer → reveal in whole-brain context → choose a side → optionally hide surrounding anatomy → restore context. These capabilities use the existing named group and retain its missing-ontology limitation.

Three return meanings: previous selection restores selection history; whole-brain restoration restores context while retaining identity; homepage return restores the entry view. None navigates to /lab.

Feedback: hover is temporary and restrained; selected highlighting persists. Rotation is distinguished from a click by the entire gesture path, and multi-touch/scroll cancellation suppresses selection. Labels name only supported regions. Unsupported foreground anatomy prevents picking through it.

Reduced motion: apply final framing, visibility, and layout without animated travel. Preserve the same information and reverse actions. Direct user manipulation remains available.
