Review sequence
  1. Discovery: compare unselected and selected views. The stage and camera controls should retain the same position and dimensions; the selected mesh changes color while surrounding tissue remains opaque. Inspect the integrated name, explanation, and Jelajahi lebih dalam action. Jelajahi anatomi switches the stable contextual panel; on mobile, Pilih anatomi above the stage provides a shortcut. Tampilkan area interaktif marks only the six supported surface meshes; violet remains the selection color. Navigator hover/focus previews the corresponding mesh without selecting it. Current selection is marked; choosing a row or pressing Escape closes the panel and restores focus to the trigger.
  2. Mobile: inspect the model, grouped controls, and information stacked below them. The compact navigator switches the same reading region and never overlays the model. Check that opening it and entering Explorer do not move the stage. Wheel-scrolling over the model should scroll the page without changing its zoom; buttons and +/− remain available. Touch pinch remains available after activating rotation. Inspect at 390 px, then open independently and resize to 320 px; check header wrapping and horizontal overflow. Tap while rotation is off; vertical swipes should scroll without selecting. Activate Aktifkan putar to rotate/pinch, then release with Selesai memutar. A drag or multi-touch gesture must not select a region.
  3. Explorer: compare unselected and selected views. Read identity → explanation → relevant actions → optional sources. Open Jelajahi anatomi to switch from precentral to postcentral, then use Pilihan sebelumnya: identity and camera should return. Below the camera controls, Pulihkan otak utuh restores context but retains identity. Kembali ke Discovery leaves Explorer and restores the entry selection/view. Reach history/return states through actual controls, not query-string simulations.
  4. Hippocampus: choose it in the Explorer menu, reveal it deliberately, switch sides, isolate, and use Pilihan sebelumnya to return to the earlier side/view. Isolate again, then Pulihkan otak utuh; surrounding anatomy must reappear without leaving Explorer. These controls do not appear for surface gyri. Previous selection can return to the earlier surface region.
